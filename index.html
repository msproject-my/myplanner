How to Install as an App

Android (Chrome / Samsung Internet)
  1. Open the link in Google Chrome on your phone.
  2. Tap the three dots icon (⋮) at the top right.
  3. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. PoliPlanner will now appear as an app icon on your home screen!
iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  1. Open the link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon (square with an arrow pointing up at the bottom).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" ().
  4. Tap "Add" at the top right.
Office PC / Laptop (Chrome / Microsoft Edge)
  1. Open the link in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Look at the address bar (URL bar) on the right side and click the Install App icon ().
  3. Or click Settings (⋮) > Apps > Install PoliPlanner.
  4. It will launch in its own dedicated, clean window without browser tabs!
